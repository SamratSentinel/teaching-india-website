Who We Are
Born from the belief that every child deserves a fair start.

Teaching India is a non-profit organisation working at the intersection of education and employment. We believe that the most impactful change happens not at the top, but at the very root — with students who stand at the crossroads of Class 10 and 12, uncertain of their path forward.

We provide structured academic support, career counselling, skill-building workshops, and mentorship that connects young learners from underserved communities with the tools and confidence they need to enter the workforce with dignity.

Our work is quiet, deliberate, and long-term. We don't measure success in events held, but in lives redirected.

What We Do
Programs built for the real world.
Every program is designed around one question: what does a student actually need to get a job and keep it?
01
Board Exam Readiness
Intensive academic support for Class 10 and 12 students in core subjects — Maths, Science, English — with a focus on passing rates and grade improvement.
Class 10 & 12
02
Career Compass
Structured career counselling that helps students understand their aptitude, explore vocational and degree pathways, and set a realistic roadmap for life after school.
Counselling
03
Workplace Skills Lab
Practical workshops in communication, digital literacy, financial basics, interview preparation, and professional conduct — skills school doesn't teach.
Life Skills
04
Mentor Network
One-on-one mentorship pairing students with working professionals from similar backgrounds — proof that the path they're walking has been walked before.
Mentorship
How We Work
A model that goes deep, not wide.
We enter a community and commit. Our approach is slow by design — because real change takes time, trust, and presence.
1
Identify
We partner with government and low-income schools to find students at the critical Class 9–10 juncture who need structured support.
2
Assess
Each student is assessed for academic gaps, aptitude, and personal circumstances. No generic interventions — only what each child actually needs.
3
Support
We deploy tutoring, counselling, skills workshops, and mentorship over a 12–24 month engagement period.
4
Place
Through our employer and vocational partner network, we actively facilitate job placements and college admissions for graduating students.
