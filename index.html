Who We Are

Closing the gap between potential and opportunity.

Teaching India was founded on a simple conviction: that a child's circumstances at birth should not determine the ceiling of their ambition. Across India, millions of bright students lack access to quality tutoring, career guidance, and professional networks that their peers in privileged schools take for granted.

Our structured 6–12 month programs pair underprivileged students with trained volunteer mentors — working professionals and educators — who deliver consistent, personalised support through to board exams, career readiness, and first employment.

We work directly with schools, colleges, and local communities to identify students who would benefit most, and we hold ourselves accountable through measurable outcomes: exam pass rates, job placements, and long-term tracking.

How It Works

A structured path from enrolment to outcome.

Every student follows the same rigorous 6–12 month journey, with progress tracked at each stage.

1
Identify & Enrol

Partner schools and community referrals nominate eligible students. We assess needs and match students to the right program.

2
Mentor Match

Each student is matched with a volunteer mentor based on subject need, geography, and personality. We train every mentor before they begin.

3
6–12 Month Program

Weekly structured sessions — online or in-person — with progress tracked monthly. Parents receive updates. No one falls through the cracks.

4
Outcome & Beyond

Exam results, job placement, or college admission marks the close. We track alumni for 12 months to measure long-term impact.
