Workplace Skills Lab

Job Seekers 6–12 Months Soft Skills & Digital Literacy 100+ Jobs Secured
About This Program

Marks open doors. Skills get you through them.

India produces millions of graduates every year, but a persistent skills gap means that employers struggle to find candidates who can communicate clearly, work in professional environments, and manage their own finances. For students from low-income backgrounds with limited exposure to corporate culture, this gap is even wider.

The Workplace Skills Lab is Teaching India's practical soft-skills program — a 6–12 month engagement designed for students and young graduates who are preparing to enter the workforce. Sessions are run by working professionals from relevant industries, not by career coaches reading from textbooks.

Participants leave with a polished resume, practiced interview technique, the ability to write professional emails, a basic digital skills toolkit, and enough financial literacy to manage their first salary. They also leave with a reference from their Teaching India mentor — and where possible, a warm introduction to our hiring partner network.

What We Teach

Six modules. Real-world skills.

📄
Resume & Cover Letter

Build a resume that gets read, not filed. Taught by actual hiring managers.

  • Resume structure and formatting
  • Writing achievements, not duties
  • Tailoring applications to job descriptions
  • Cover letter writing
🎤
Interview Preparation

Practice until it feels natural. Mock interviews, feedback, and technique.

  • Answering common interview questions
  • The STAR method for competency questions
  • Body language and presence
  • Questions to ask the interviewer
✉️
Professional Communication

Email, meetings, feedback, and workplace relationships — done right.

  • Writing clear professional emails
  • How to give and receive feedback
  • Communicating with managers and clients
  • Workplace etiquette and culture
💻
Digital Skills

The baseline digital toolkit every job requires in 2025.

  • Google Workspace / Microsoft Office
  • Basic data entry and spreadsheets
  • Professional LinkedIn profile
  • Online safety and digital citizenship
💰
Financial Literacy

Understanding your first salary, savings, and financial independence.

  • Understanding your payslip
  • Budgeting your first salary
  • Introduction to savings and banking
  • Avoiding debt traps and scams
🤝
Networking & Job Search

Finding opportunities — and being found — in a competitive market.

  • Where to look for entry-level jobs
  • Building a professional network
  • Government job portals and schemes
  • Warm introductions from Teaching India mentors
How It's Delivered

Workshops, coaching, and real practice.

The Workplace Skills Lab is different from a classroom — it's structured around practice, not lectures. Sessions are run by working professionals who've actually hired people, managed teams, and navigated the same challenges our students are facing.

🎓
Monthly Group Workshops90-minute interactive sessions on each module, with exercises and peer discussion
👤
1:1 CoachingIndividual sessions for resume review, mock interviews, and personalised feedback
💬
WhatsApp Support GroupA facilitated peer group for questions, job leads, and encouragement between sessions
🔗
Hiring Partner IntroductionsWhere applicable, warm introductions to our network of hiring partners for entry-level roles
