Board Exam Readiness

Structured 6–12 month tutoring in Maths, Science, and English for Class X and XII students. Past papers, mock tests, exam strategy — so no student faces the board alone.

Class X & XII 6–12 Months CBSE · ICSE · State Boards 90%+ Pass Rate
About This Program

Every student deserves a second pair of eyes on their syllabus.

The board exam is one of the most consequential moments in a young Indian's life — yet for millions of students in government and low-fee private schools, there is no after-school support, no tuition money, and no guidance on how to study effectively. Teaching India's Board Exam Readiness program exists to close that gap.

Each student is matched with a trained volunteer tutor who commits to a minimum 6-month engagement. Tutors are educated working professionals or postgraduates who have been through the CBSE or ICSE system themselves. We train every tutor before they begin, provide a structured syllabus calendar, and check in monthly to ensure progress is on track.

The program runs across Maths, Science, and English — the three subjects that most determine Class X and XII outcomes. Students also receive mock tests, past paper analysis, and a final exam strategy session in the weeks before boards.

What We Teach

Three core subjects. Full syllabus coverage.

📐
Mathematics
  • Algebra & Quadratic Equations
  • Trigonometry & Coordinate Geometry
  • Statistics & Probability
  • Calculus (Class XII)

  • Step-by-step marks maximisation

English
  • Grammar: Tenses, Voice, Narration

  • Essay writing for high scores
  • Board-specific answer format coaching
Program Journey

6–12 months, structured from day one.

1
Month 1
Enrolment & Assessment

Student enrolled through a partner school or community referral. Baseline assessment to identify gaps. Matched with a suitable tutor within 2 weeks.

2
Months 2–4
Foundation & Concept Building

Weekly tutoring sessions covering syllabus concepts. Tutor follows the shared curriculum calendar, adapting pace to the student's needs. Monthly progress check-ins.

3
Months 5–8
Practice & Reinforcement

Past papers, mock tests, and timed practice. Students build exam confidence and get detailed tutor feedback on answer quality and marks maximisation.

4
Final Weeks
Revision & Exam Strategy

Intensive revision of high-weightage topics. Final exam strategy session: reading question papers, time management, attempting for maximum marks. Tutor available through exam week.

✓
Post-Exam
Results & Transition

We celebrate results with students and families. Graduates referred to Career Compass. We track outcomes for 12 months post-program.
